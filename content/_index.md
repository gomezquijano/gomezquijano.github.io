        ## About Me

        I am an **evolutionary biologist and molecular ecologist** interested in how evolutionary and ecological processes interact to shape the evolutionary trajectories of natural plant populations. My research combines **population genomics** and **quantitative genetics** to investigate local adaptation, demographic history, and biological invasions.

        I am currently a **Postdoctoral Fellow** in the Department of Ecology & Evolutionary Biology at the **University of Toronto**, where I work in the **Wright Lab** on a collaborative project investigating the invasion history and genetic basis of adaptation in **capeweed (*Arctotheca calendula*)** across Australia. My current research focuses on **temporal population genomics**, using herbarium collections to reconstruct patterns of introduction, spread, and adaptation through time.

        I completed my **PhD in Biology** at **Queen's University** in 2026 under the supervision of **Dr. Rob Colautti**. Previously, I earned an **MSc in Integrated Biosciences** from the **University of Minnesota Duluth** in 2021, where I was co-supervised by **Dr. Briana Gross** and **Dr. Julie Etterson**.

        Beyond research, I am deeply committed to **teaching, mentorship, anti-oppressive approaches to science education, and science communication**. These have been central to my academic training, and I strive to incorporate them into every aspect of my work.
