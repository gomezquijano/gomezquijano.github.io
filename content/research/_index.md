    content:
      title: "Temporal Genomics and Adaptation"
      subtitle: "Arctotheca calendula | Capeweed"
      text: |-
        How do populations evolve following introductions to new environments?

        I use contemporary and historical genomic data, including herbarium specimens, to reconstruct the demographic history of capeweed and investigate how genetic diversity and adaptation have changed across space and time.

        This research explores the contributions of genetic drift, gene flow, and natural selection to evolutionary change.

      title: "Population Genomics for Ecological Restoration"
      subtitle: "Calamagrostis species | Native Grasses"
      text: |-
        How can genomic information improve ecological restoration practices?

        My research uses genomic tools to investigate genetic differentiation, hybridization, and relationships between wild and commercially sourced populations of native grasses in northern Canada.

        These findings help inform species identification and the selection of appropriate plant material for restoration.
